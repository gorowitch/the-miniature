# Writing Panel Manifests for De Miniatuur

The final images are generated by Copilot from text in `the-miniature-cartoon-bible.md`. Two recurring failures drove the manifest format:
- **The wrong character appears to speak** (e.g. Alfil seems to say the King's lines), because image generators guess the speaker from the composition.
- **Balloon text drifts from the Flemish script**, because generators paraphrase long passages.

A manifest fixes, in writing and before any image exists, who speaks, who listens, where every tail points, and the exact text. Read Section 19 (Dialogue Attribution) and Section 20 (template and worked example 5b) of the bible before writing; they are the contract.

Changes to the bible follow the discipline of the `miniature-bible-change` skill (snapshot, additive edits, verification, log). Use its verification script at the end.

## Input
A scene number (Section 17), a range of scenes, or specific panels. If the user just says "next scene", take the first scene in the Script Coverage Map (Section 22.E) whose manifests are not done.

## Workflow

### 1. Gather the sources
Read from the bible:
- Section 1: the play lines for the scene (with speaker labels and stage directions).
- Section 17: the scene catalog entry (position, characters, panels, camera notes).
- Section 18: existing panel blocks for the scene, if any. These are approved or proposed by the author; they are the source for the manifests, so keep their camera, framing and staging decisions instead of inventing new ones.
- Section 12: the board state(s) for the scene.
- Sections 7, 13, 14, 19, 20, 22.E: dossiers, rules, forbidden elements, attribution rules, template, coverage map.
- The manifests of the previous scene, for continuity.

### 2. Split the scene into panels (only if Section 18 has none)
- Default: one speaker per panel (Section 19.D). Split long speeches over several panels at natural sentence boundaries; every part keeps the same owner and the original wording.
- Stage directions get their own panel when they are an action ("(Alfil overlegt met pionnen en komt terug)"); "(zucht)" is a pose of the speaker, not a separate panel.
- Played moves (e.g. "1. e3") change the board state between panels; note which panel shows the position before and after.
- Mark new panel splits as "Status: proposal, awaiting author review" - the author approves splits and staging.

### 3. Fill all 13 fields per panel
Use the Section 20.1 template exactly. Points that are easy to get wrong:
- **Field 3, dialogue**: copy from Section 1. Only allowed change: collapse runs of spaces. Keep straight and curly quotes, spelling, "!", "?", " :" spacing and chess notation as written.
- **Field 4, speaker**: the name before the line in the script (Koning, Alfil, Regina, Caballo) - never the most prominent character. The Black King never speaks; his draw offer is reported by Caballo.
- **Field 6, listeners**: every visible listener has a closed mouth and a reaction that follows from the line (surprise at news, worry when plans fail, offence at an insult). A listener's mouth may be open only for surprise, stated explicitly, and then nothing points to them.
- **Field 8, gaze**: pieces that talk to each other look at each other; the listener looks back.
- **Field 9, tails**: the tail ends at the speaker's mouth and does not pass close to a listener's head. Reading order (top-left first) follows the script order.
- **Field 11, board**: the State number from Section 12, plus the move if one happens.
- **Field 12, continuity**: carry forward what earlier panels established (who has moved, who is captured, the King's mood).
- **Field 13, must not appear**: at least an open mouth or balloon on the listeners, invented text, moved pieces, and any scene-specific trap.

### 4. Chess and canon traps to check
- Seen from Black's side the files run h (left) to a (right); from White's side a to h. State left-to-right order explicitly in field 7.
- Every visible occupied square shows its piece, also at frame edges; nothing on empty squares.
- Alfil cannot leave f1 until 2. Ld3 (e2 and g2 block him).
- Knights always face the centre files (d, e).
- The cola stain on f6 is only faint until Scene 15; the greasy spot on f3 is faint; the boebel is a warped rise of the board itself.
- Regina is captured by 6...gxh5 and afterwards stands beside the board, looking at the black rook on h8.
- Only content from the script or decided by the author; staging (pose, gaze, expression) is allowed when it supports the line.

### 5. Put the manifests in the bible
- Add them to Section 20 under a heading per scene, e.g. `## 20.3 Manifests: Scene 6 - Regina's Advice`, after the existing manifest subsections and before Section 21. Each manifest in a fenced block, like the worked example.
- Update the Status column of the Script Coverage Map (Section 22.E) for every script item the scene covers.
- Do not change Section 18 panel blocks unless the author asks; if a manifest reveals a mistake in a Section 18 block (e.g. a camera note that no longer matches an approved sketch), report it.

### 6. Verify
Run:
```bash
bash .claude/skills/miniature-bible-change/scripts/verify-bible.sh <snapshot> the-miniature-cartoon-bible.md
```
Every "Balloon N:" line in the new manifests is checked against the play. Also check yourself that every script item of the scene appears in exactly one manifest (no line dropped, none duplicated) and in the original order.

### 7. Report
For each panel give a one-line summary (speaker -> line start, who listens, camera), then the verification results, the open questions, and what the author must approve. Offer a quick layout sketch only for panels whose camera is hard to judge from text; sketches are previews, not deliverables, and must be checked in a browser before showing them.

## Example of the core of a manifest
```
3.  Balloon 1: "Jamaar Sire, met dieje boebel."
4.  Balloon 1 -> Alfil
6.  Listening: Koning, mouth closed, frowning, looks at Alfil.
9.  Balloon 1 top left, tail to Alfil's mouth; does not cross Koning's head.
11. State 2 (after 1. ... d5). No move in this panel.
13. Must not appear: an open mouth on Koning; a tail near Koning; Alfil off f1.
```
