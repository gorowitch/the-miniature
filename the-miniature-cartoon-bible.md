# SECTION 2 - ESTABLISHED CANON

## Visual Style
- Belgian comic style.
- Medieval kingdom atmosphere.
- Family friendly.
- Bright colours.
- Expressive faces.

## Scale
- Chess-piece scale.
- Small defects create major drama.

## Anthropomorphism
- Classic chess-piece bodies.
- Faces allowed.
- Eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, moustaches.
- Simple arms and hands.
- No feet.
- Visible felt bottoms.

# SECTION 3 - BOARD CANON

## Board
- Plastic board.
- Several years old.
- Worn but functional.

## Boebel
Squares:
- d4
- e4
- d5
- e5

Rules:
- Board surface itself is warped.
- Not a separate bubble.
- Not a dome.
- Four squares form one raised region.

## Cola Stain
- Square f6.
- Covers most of the square.
- Critical plot element.

## Paprika Rule
- Paprika stains only on Alfil's mitre.
- No paprika crumbs on the board.

# SECTION 4 - CHARACTER DOSSIERS

## White King
- Ancient veteran.
- Pristine despite age.
- Simple medieval crown.
- Beard and moustache.
- Proud.
- Nostalgic.
- Strategic.
- Impatient.
- Cannot admit mistakes.
- Constantly talking and gesturing.

## Black King (Dieje van den Overkant)
- Same age as White King.
- Short beard.
- Calm.
- Disciplined.
- Professional.
- Patient.
- Competent counterpart.
- Long-time rival.

## Regina
- Elegant and regal.
- Voice of reason.
- Recurring Black Rook gag.

## Alfil
- Advisor.
- Scholar.
- Church figure.
- Diplomatic.
- Paprika stains on mitre.

## Caballo
- Heroic knight.
- Brave.
- Loyal.
- Refuses dirty squares.
- Protects his felt bottom.

## White Pawns
- Soldiers.
- Slight individual differences.
- More realistic faces.

## Black Pawns
- Similar to White pawns.

## Black Rook
- Specifically the rook on h8.
- Handsome military officer.

# SECTION 5 - RELATIONSHIPS

- White King = expressive chaos.
- Black King = controlled competence.
- Long-time rivals with mutual respect.
- Alfil is mediator.
- Regina is repeatedly right.

# SECTION 6 - RUNNING GAGS

- The boebel.
- Dirty squares.
- Cola stain.
- Felt bottoms.
- Old wooden boards.
- Regina and the black rook.
