# ORIGINAL STORY

De Miniatuur (Een hedendaagse klucht in één bedrijf)

Koning:
Wat is het vandaag toch een heuglijke dag. Aanschouw mijn troepen, van toren tot toren staan ze in perfecte slagorde opgesteld, klaar voor deze dappere strijd. Dat "Dieje van den overkant" al maar zeker is dat hij nu al begint te bibberen. Want, ik voel het aan mijn viltje, deze roemrijke partij win ik vandaag ! Alfil, zijn ook onze pionnen klaar voor deze heldhaftige strijd ?

Alfil:
Jawel Sire, zij wachten op uw bevel !

Koning:
Wel laat ons dan niet langer talmen : glorie en eer wachten op ons. Ten aanval ! 1. e4

(... canonical source continues exactly from the supplied play; use the original play text as authoritative dialogue source for all comic adaptation work.)

3. No invented captions.
4. No coordinate labels on the board.
5. No extra jokes.

# WORLD RULES

- Chess pieces are alive.
- Chess pieces speak.
- Chess moves are military orders.
- Board defects are physical terrain.
- Captured pieces simply leave the board.

# BOARD GEOGRAPHY

## The Boebel

Affected squares:
- d4
- e4
- d5
- e5

Important:
The board surface itself is warped.
The boebel is NOT a separate object and NOT a dome sitting on top of the board.

## Cola Stain

Square:
- f6

The stain covers most of the square.

## Paprika

Only visible as paprika stains on Alfil's mitre.

There are no paprika chips or crumbs on the board.

# CHARACTER CANON

## White King

- Ancient veteran
- Looks pristine despite age
- Simple medieval crown
- Beard and moustache
- Proud
- Impatient
- Cannot admit mistakes
- Constantly gesturing
- Constantly talking

## Black King (Dieje van den Overkant)

- Same age as White King
- Short beard
- Calm
- Professional
- Disciplined
- Patient
- Competent counterpart to White King
- Long-time rival

## Regina

- Elegant and regal
- Voice of reason
- Usually correct
- Recurring Black Rook gag

## Alfil

- Advisor
- Scholar
- Church figure
- Diplomatic
- Paprika stains on mitre

## Caballo

- Heroic knight
- Brave
- Loyal
- Refuses dirty squares

## White Pawns

- Soldiers
- Slight visual differences
- More realistic faces
- No blob-like cartoon faces

## Black Pawns

- Similar to White pawns

## Black Rook

- Handsome military officer
- Object of Regina's recurring interest

# POSITION TABLE

Initial position: standard chess starting position.

After 1.e3:
White pawn e2 -> e3.

After 1...d5:
Black pawn d7 -> d5.

After 2.Bd3:
White bishop f1 -> d3.

After 2...e6:
Black pawn e7 -> e6.

After 3.Nh3:
White knight g1 -> h3.

After 3...Bd7:
Black bishop c8 -> d7.

After 4.Qh5:
White queen d1 -> h5.

After 4...Ne7:
Black knight g8 -> e7.

After 5.Ng5:
White knight h3 -> g5.

After 5...g6:
Black pawn g7 -> g6.

After 6.Nxh7:
White knight captures h7.

After 6...gxh5:
Black pawn captures White queen on h5.
